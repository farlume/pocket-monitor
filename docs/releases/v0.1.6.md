## 简体中文

- 四套可切换参考配色：Keychron C1 黑白、K2 灰红、Akko 黑金、Varmilo 海韵蓝白。布局和配色独立选择，连同震动偏好一起保存。
- 立体键帽有斜面、阴影、按下下沉和松开回弹；每次按下提供可关闭的轻触震动。点按修饰键键帽或选择条可设置一次组合键，选中键帽亮起，发送后自动清除。

- Fn 为应用内导航层：方向键对应 Home/End/PgUp/PgDn，退格对应 Del，Del 对应 Ins；先松开 Fn 仍正确松开原发送键。K2 灯光键控制界面灯效，不模拟品牌硬件固件、媒体功能或 RGB 协议。

## English

- Adds physical ANSI layouts: 87-key TKL and Keychron K2-style compact 84-key. Key positions, spacing and widths follow the physical boards, including a 6.25u space bar, separate TKL navigation cluster, inverted-T arrows and the K2's 1.75u right Shift.
- Four independent color themes: Keychron C1 ivory, K2 graphite/red, Akko Black & Gold and Varmilo Sea Melody-inspired blue/white. Layout, theme and vibration preferences are saved.
- Sculpted caps depress and rebound with shrinking shadows and optional haptic feedback. Tap modifier caps or chips to latch a one-shot shortcut; selected caps highlight and clear after sending.
- Adds fullscreen typing and whole-board scaling, retaining larger-key horizontal scrolling, the optional number pad and multi-touch holds. Changing layouts, closing fullscreen or leaving the input panel clears input.
- App-local Fn maps arrows to Home/End/Page Up/Page Down, Backspace to Delete and Delete to Insert; releasing Fn first still releases the originally sent key. The K2 lamp toggles visual lighting, without emulating brand firmware, media functions or RGB protocols.
- Includes four actual Compose UI theme previews and bilingual documentation. Geometry and colors are references; brand logos and product illustrations are not included.

Local validation passed 21 shared tests, 79 Android/Robolectric/Compose tests and 10 Python packaging tests, plus the Debug build and Lint. The tag release workflow performs signed Release builds and signature/file verification.

**Hardware validation remains pending:** vibration, phone multi-touch, Bluetooth host input, Mac keyboard setup and simultaneous HDMI capture. The reported OPPO PLG110 / Android 16 with UGREEN 95348 (USB `2b89:5348`) may stop delivering frames after format changes and require reconnecting; this release does not claim to fix it.
